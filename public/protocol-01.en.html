PROTOCOL #01: Wild Contaminated Water Purification & Ingestion Guide

Drinking untreated wild water can lead to fatal bacterial dysentery and giardiasis, resulting in death from dehydration within 72 hours. Water must undergo mechanical active charcoal filtration followed by compulsory thermal boiling.

소요 시간
15 MINUTES
생존율/성공률
99.8% (Post-Boiling)
기술 난이도
Tier 1 (Primitive Bushcraft)
시간당 생산량
1.5 Liters / Hour

REQ 필수 확보 물자 체크리스트 (즉시 수집할 것)

0/3 확보 완료
대체 여과 차단재: 깨끗한 면 손수건, 건조된 물이끼, 또는 불에 그을린 자작나무 껍질 메쉬 [야전 검증 완료]

실행 절차 // 수직 낙하식 야전 여과 파이프라인

1단계 ~ 3단계 진행
그림: Vessel Preparation & Filter Base Assembly
01

Vessel Preparation & Filter Base Assembly

Using a sharp survival knife, cut 3 inches (7-8cm) off the bottom of a plastic bottle to form a wide funnel. Invert with spout facing down.

> Action: Pierce 3 small pinholes in the cap, or tightly pack cotton cloth/sphagnum moss to prevent filtration medium loss.

그림: Layering the Filter Media (Inverted Pyramid Hierarchy)
02

Layering the Filter Media (Inverted Pyramid Hierarchy)

Strictly stack layers in reverse order: Spout cloth plug -> 2.5 inches (6cm) crushed charcoal -> 2 inches (5cm) fine sand -> 1.2 inches (3cm) coarse gravel.

> Science: Gravel blocks floating leaves, sand captures particulates, and porous activated charcoal adsorbs organic toxins and odors.

그림: Pouring Turbid Water & Clear Water Collection
03

Pouring Turbid Water & Clear Water Collection

Slowly pour murky water onto the top gravel bed. Gravity will draw water through the column at roughly one steady drop per second into a clean container.

> Warning: Discard the initial 250ml containing loose charcoal dust. Continue running water until output runs crystal clear.

위험 // 치명적 실수 경고 음용 전 필독
⚠️

FATAL MISTAKE: Never drink filtered water directly without boiling.

Mechanical charcoal filtration only removes sediments, heavy metals, and odors. Viruses, Giardia cysts, and Cryptosporidium easily pass through. Filtered water MUST be boiled at a rolling boil for at least 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft elevation) to ensure total survival safety.

Consequence of skipping boil: Acute Cryptosporidiosis infection and lethal dehydration within 96 hours. [타협 불가한 절대 수칙]
